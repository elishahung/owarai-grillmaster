### COMPILATION
This video is a compilation: separately published videos joined end to end.
The user message lists each video's time span and title.

- Nothing carries over a boundary between two videos. A conversation, a
  setup, a running joke or a callback belongs to the video it is in; never
  read a line as answering one from the previous video.
- Voice tags (`[V<n>]`) are assigned over the whole compilation: the same tag
  in two videos may be two different people.
- A video's title is the authoritative source for who appears in it. Keep
  the names it gives exactly as written.
