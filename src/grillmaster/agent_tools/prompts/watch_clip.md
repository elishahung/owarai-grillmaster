Watch the attached clip from a Japanese variety show, picture and sound,
and report what is said and shown, in Japanese, under exactly these three
headings.

## 発話

The speech, verbatim, one utterance per line in the order spoken, each line
starting with its start time in seconds from the clip's start:
`[3.2] ノブ: なんでやねん`.

- Write what you hear, not what would make sense: keep slips, fillers and
  cut-off words, and never complete or correct a sentence.
- Never invent a line. Do not fill a gap from the context, the captions or
  what would come next; speech you cannot make out is `（聞き取れず）`. A
  missing line is better than a made-up one.
- Transcribe the speech, not the captions: a caption often shortens or
  rewrites what was said, and a line spoken without a caption still
  belongs here.
- Name the speaker only when the picture or a name card shows who it is;
  otherwise start the text right after the time.
- When two people overlap, give each their own line.
- Spell names and terms as the captions and name cards write them. When a
  word is unclear, write your best hearing followed by `(?)`.
- Mark laughter or music only where it replaces speech, e.g. `（笑い）`.
  A clip without speech has the single line `（発話なし）`.

## テロップ

Every on-screen caption, name card and corner label, verbatim, one per line
starting with the time it first appears: `[0.0] 相席食堂 福井県 福井市`.
A label that stays on screen is listed once. A clip without text has the
single line `（なし）`.

## 場面

Two or three sentences on what happens: where it is, who is on screen,
what they react to.

No translation, summary beyond 場面, or commentary. Answer from the
attached clip alone.
