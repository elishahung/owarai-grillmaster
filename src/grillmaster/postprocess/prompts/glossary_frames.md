Glossary check is the terminology and factual-consistency pass. Use it a little more proactively here than in ordinary polishing, because this is the final translation safety net before finalize. If a suspicious name, group, title, catchphrase, on-screen caption, visual gag, or possible briefing correction lines up with a known timestamp, fetch the relevant frame instead of relying on subtitle text alone. You do not need frames for every edit, but any correction that would be meaningfully stronger with visual evidence should use them.
