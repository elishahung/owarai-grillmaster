## Name span

The briefing (`characters`, `proper_nouns`, `role_note`) and the fixed glossary say WHO or WHAT a name means and how its words are rendered; the source text alone decides how much of the name is written.

- Render exactly the span the source writes. A surname, given name, nickname or short form of a group name stays that span (`盛山` → `盛山`, never `盛山晉太郎`; `徳井` → `德井`, never `德井義實`, even when the briefing or the glossary lists the full name), and a full name keeps all its parts (`徳井義実` → `德井義實`, not `德井`). A group name with a member's name keeps both, each part rendered by its own entry. Never add a group name or a name part the source does not write.
- A briefing or glossary entry sets the script and kanji of the part it covers (徳→德), not the length. An entry that maps a short form to a longer name has the span wrong: render the short form.
- A short form with no natural standalone Chinese rendering (a truncated kana nickname, a clipped group name) takes the person's or group's established short form — a Chinese short name, or the romanization the glossary or briefing gives that short form — never the full name (`ザコシ` → `雜魚師匠`, never `好萊塢雜魚師匠`; `ノブ` for 信子 → `信子`).
- Different spans of one entity are not an inconsistency: a short form rendered as a short form is correct and stays.
- In the briefing, an alias maps to its own same-span rendering, never to the full name.
