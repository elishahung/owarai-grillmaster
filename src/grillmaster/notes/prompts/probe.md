## Dry run

This run checks how you decide; it writes no notes. Do not search the web or look up facts. Go through the whole episode and list in `candidates` everything you weighed against the rules above: its `block` (the block a note would show with) and `term` (copied exactly as it is written in that block's `ja` or `zh`), `annotate` true when you would write a note for it, and in `reason` one sentence in Traditional Chinese (Taiwan): what the viewer is missing, or what already explains it.
